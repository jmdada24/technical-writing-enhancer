# Clarity

## Definition
Clarity in technical writing refers to presenting ideas in a clear, organized, and unambiguous manner so the reader can easily understand who is doing what, why, and how. Clear writing avoids confusing sentence structures, misplaced modifiers, vague references, and disorganized thoughts.

A clear sentence allows the reader to immediately identify:
- the subject (who or what)
- the action (what is happening)
- the object or result (to whom or what)

## Key Principles
- Sentences should clearly show the subject, action, and object.
- Related words must be placed close to the words they modify.
- Avoid sentence structures that allow multiple interpretations.
- Each sentence should express one main idea.
- Ideas should follow a logical and natural order.

## Common Clarity Problems

### Misplaced Modifiers
A misplaced modifier occurs when a descriptive phrase is placed too far from the word it modifies, causing ambiguity or unintended meaning.

**Common pattern:**
- Passive verb + by PERSON + location phrase

This structure may cause the reader to misinterpret who or what the location phrase refers to.

### Unclear Actor
Sentences written in the passive voice may obscure who is responsible for the action.

### Ambiguous References
Using pronouns or introductory phrases without clear referents can confuse the reader.

### Overloaded Sentences
Packing multiple ideas into one sentence reduces clarity and increases cognitive load.

## Examples

### Poor (Misplaced Modifier)
The contract should be signed by Mr. Aguirre on the dotted line.

### Improved
The contract should be signed on the dotted line by Mr. Aguirre.

---

### Poor (Unclear Subject)
As newcomers to our community, the merchants of ABC Company wish to make available a variety of complimentary products and services.

### Improved
As newcomers to our community, you are invited to take advantage of a variety of complimentary products and services offered by the merchants of ABC Company.

---

### Poor (Ambiguous Reference)
After reviewing the report, it was approved.

### Improved
After reviewing the report, the manager approved it.

---

### Poor (Multiple Ideas)
The system was updated yesterday and users should restart their devices because performance issues were reported.

### Improved
The system was updated yesterday. Users should restart their devices to resolve reported performance issues.

## When to Apply Clarity
Apply clarity when:
- The meaning can be misunderstood
- The sentence structure allows multiple interpretations
- The reader may misinterpret who performs the action
- A modifier could logically attach to more than one word
- The sentence feels confusing even if grammatically correct

## Diagnostic Questions
Ask these questions to test clarity:
- Can the reader immediately tell who is doing what?
- Could any phrase modify the wrong word?
- Would two readers interpret this sentence the same way?
