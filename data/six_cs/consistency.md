# Consistency

## Definition
Consistency in technical writing refers to the uniform use of terminology, titles, formatting, references, and numerical expressions throughout a document. Consistent writing helps readers avoid confusion and builds trust in the accuracy and professionalism of the message.

Consistency does not improve meaning directly, but it prevents misunderstanding caused by variation.

## Key Principles
- Use the same word or term for the same concept throughout a document.
- Maintain consistent titles, names, and references.
- Apply uniform formatting to headings, lists, and sections.
- Present numbers, dates, and units in a consistent style.

## Common Consistency Problems

### Inconsistent Titles or Names
Switching between different titles or names for the same person or entity.

Examples:
- Ms. Guzman / Mrs. Guzman
- ABC Corp / ABC Corporation

### Terminology Switching
Using multiple terms for the same concept.

Examples:
- “contract” and “agreement” used interchangeably
- “employee” and “staff member” referring to the same group

### Number and Date Formatting
Using different styles for similar data.

Examples:
- “ten employees” vs. “10 employees”
- “June 15, 2024” vs. “15 June 2024”

### Formatting Variations
Inconsistent capitalization, headings, or list styles.

## Examples

### Inconsistent
Ms. Guzman attended the meeting. Later, Mrs. Guzman presented the report.

### Consistent
Ms. Guzman attended the meeting. Later, Ms. Guzman presented the report.

---

### Inconsistent
The agreement must be signed today. Once the contract is finalized, copies will be distributed.

### Consistent
The contract must be signed today. Once the contract is finalized, copies will be distributed.

---

### Inconsistent
The system requires five inputs. All 5 inputs must be validated.

### Consistent
The system requires five inputs. All five inputs must be validated.

## When to Apply Consistency
Apply consistency when:
- The same person, object, or concept is referenced multiple times
- Titles, names, or terms change within the same document
- Numbers, dates, or formats are mixed
- Formatting differences distract the reader

## Diagnostic Questions
Ask these questions to test consistency:
- Is the same term used for the same idea throughout?
- Are names and titles presented the same way every time?
- Are numbers and dates formatted uniformly?
- Does formatting remain consistent across sections?
